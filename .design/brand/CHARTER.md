# Watermark charter

Laws every screen and the evidence pack obey. Numbers in brackets point at
`.design/refs/NOTES.md`. The checkable part is `charter.json`, run with
`lint_brand.py web/app web/components --charter .design/brand/charter.json`.

## Colour

1. **Red is a line, never an area.** The plaque red (#B3162A light, #F0626A dark) is used for
   the high-water line, the mark's plate, the diamond on the board, and nothing larger than
   about 8 px tall. Because a red fill standing for water reads as blood (21).
2. **Water is enamel blue** (#3D6FA8 light, #5C8DC9 dark), and only water is blue. Changed
   by the owner on 2026-10-09: a gauge reads as water at a glance when the water is blue.
   Never Ring's bright cyan, never a gradient.
3. **Red is never body text.** Red marks a level or a confirmed event. Prose stays ink or muted.
4. **Enamel white is home, navy is ink.** Paper is #F0F1EE, ink is #1B2A47, the dark theme
   is the same navy as a ground (#16213A). Because Béhuard's staff is white enamel with navy
   E-marks (2, 3). No pure white or pure black.

## Type

5. **Big Shoulders only for the wordmark and numbers read off the staff; Commissioner for
   everything else, titles in its bold.** Changed 2026-10-09: a condensed display face on
   every title was read as a generated pairing in every critique round.
6. **Sentence case everywhere.** No all-caps headings. At most two small tracked labels per
   screen, and only on data (units, a column head). Because tracked caps above every heading
   is the template this replaces.
7. **Titles carry no full stop and no comma.** A question keeps its question mark.
8. **Numbers are tabular and carry their unit** (6.7 s, 14:02:11, 4 frames).

## The signature device: the high-water line

9. **Every record of water gets one high-water line**: a red rule with a rivet at each end,
   what happened above it, the date and time below it (1). One per record, never decorative,
   never on a screen with no water.
10. **History is a board.** Past incidents sit as diamonds at their heights on a staff with a
    tag pointing at each, newest on top (2). Never a card grid.

## Material

11. **Illustrations are prints.** They come from `art/` only, made with the art direction in
    `art-direction.md`, and sit directly on the ground with no disc, card or shadow behind
    them. Empty, error and setup states each have one.
12. **Photos are my real floods or Ring frames, labelled.** Never a generated image
    presented as a photo, never stock. Every Ring frame shows its Ring timestamp.
13. **The limestone grain is on every page ground**, at low opacity, never over text blocks.
14. **No icon library.** Icons come from the brand set: 24 grid, 1.75 stroke, square caps,
    one rivet dot each.

## Composition and motion

15. **One strong object per screen**: the gauge, the board, the frame strip, the pack. Chrome
    stays quiet so the object can be loud.
16. **One orchestrated moment per screen**: water rising in detents, a notch dropping, a plaque
    line drawing across. Nothing moves on exit, everything rests under reduced motion.

## Voice

17. **I speak plainly, to a homeowner who just had water on the floor.** Short sentences,
    real times, no reassurance I cannot back (see `voice.md`).
18. **Never Ring's mark.** No Ring logo, no Ring wordmark styling, no Ring cyan as our
    colour, no badge implying endorsement (Ring Appstore Program Requirements, section 3).
    "Ring" appears only as plain text naming the devices and the API.
19. **Honest labels stay visible**: "Controlled test. Real device.", "Simulated sensor",
    "This record cannot vouch for". They are never shrunk, hidden or styled as fine print.
