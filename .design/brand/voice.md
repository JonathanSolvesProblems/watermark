# Voice

**Who speaks:** me, someone who has had water on the floor twice, talking to a homeowner
whose sensor just went off. Calm, specific, no sales.

## Sample lines

- Headline: When water reaches the sensor, the camera is already looking
- Button: Send the pack
- Empty: No marks yet. The sensor is dry.
- Error: Ring didn't answer. Nothing was lost, I'll try again in a minute.
- Confirmation: Pack sent to your adjuster at 14:06.

## Banned phrases

- peace of mind
- smart home
- seamless
- AI-powered
- something went wrong

## Vocabulary

Say: water, sensor, camera, frame, evidence pack, adjuster, claim, room, the time.
Never on screen: webhook, incident state machine, WHEP, payload, capture pipeline, Bedrock,
model names, "Pillar".
